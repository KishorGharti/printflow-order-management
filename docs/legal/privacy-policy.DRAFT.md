## 1. Who we are

This policy covers the PrintFlow order management service operated by [[legal business name]], [[registered address]], [[registration / PAN number]] ("we", "us").
Contact for privacy questions: [[email address]], [[phone number]].

The service is used by print shops ("shops") to manage their orders, clients, production, stock, expenses and staff. Each shop decides what information it enters about its own clients and staff.

## 2. Information the service stores

**Shop owner accounts:** name, email address, phone number, company name, password (stored as a one-way hash), subscription plan and dates, and last login time.

**Staff accounts:** name, phone number, email, role, job title, employee ID, login password or PIN, salary and advance records, and attendance records, as entered by the shop.

**A shop's client records:** name, business name, phone, WhatsApp number, email, address, PAN number, custom prices, advance balance and payment history, as entered by the shop.

**Orders and documents:** order details, quantities, prices, due dates, status history, payment records, bills, delivery notes (chalan), and design files or logos that the shop uploads.

**Business records:** expenses, vendors, stock items and stock movements, and monthly ledger reports.

**Security and activity records:** login attempts, administrative actions, the IP address and browser information of the request, and technical error reports.

## 3. How the information is used

- To provide the order, production, stock, payment and reporting features the shop uses.
- To sign users in, send email verification and password reset codes, and keep accounts secure.
- To produce bills, delivery notes, statements and reports for the shop.
- To send order and payment messages to a shop's clients over WhatsApp when the shop chooses to.
- To create backups and investigate errors.
